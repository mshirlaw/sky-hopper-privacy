Sky Hopper Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

Sky Hopper is an iPhone game. This page explains what it does with your information. The short version: it collects none.

What we collect

Nothing. Sky Hopper has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things on your device only: your best score, your coins, the upgrades and skins you own, the skin you wear, which first-run hints you have seen, and a record of purchases already given to you. This stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

Coin packs and some skins are sold as in-app purchases through Apple. Apple takes the payment and handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. We never see your card or Apple Account details. The game only receives a confirmation from Apple that a purchase happened, so it can give you what you bought. Restore Purchases, in Settings, asks Apple to confirm what you own so the game can give back skins you bought on another phone.

Third parties

The game does not use any third-party services, software development kits for analytics or advertising, or social media.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to collect from children either. Purchases need the approval settings of the Apple Account, so parents can use Apple's Family Sharing and Ask to Buy controls.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a leaderboard or sign-in is added, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
